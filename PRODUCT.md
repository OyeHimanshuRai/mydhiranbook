# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Pawn-shop (girvi / dhiran) owners and their counter staff in India. Devices, in the owner's order of importance: tablet and phone first, then laptop — every screen size must fully work, not just shrink. The owner also checks the book from a phone away from the shop. Staff are not all comfortable with software. The job: lend against gold and silver pledged by a customer, record interest and repayments accurately, hand the customer a printed receipt, and know at a glance what is due.

## Product Purpose

Dhiran is a standalone pledge-loan book for pawn shops: borrowers with KYC, pledged items, loans, interest and repayments, release/renewal/forfeiture, receipts, reports, staff roles. Shops sign up, verify email, pay for a plan (Razorpay) and run their whole business in it. Success: a clerk records a pledge or a repayment in under a minute without error, and the owner trusts the numbers.

## Positioning

Built only for the pledge-loan business (no jewellery-shop ERP around it): the loan book, the lock-period and minimum-interest rules, and the printed receipt are the product. Independent of any other product or domain.

## Operating Context

Shop counter with a PC or tablet; owner on a phone. Receipts are printed (A5/A4) and handed to customers; cash and UPI payments. Money rules are strict (interest accrual, minimum interest, lock period, settlement). Subscription plans are paid yearly or monthly, no free trial, prices in INR.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Indian formats everywhere: rupee amounts with lakh grouping, dd Mon yyyy dates, 10-digit mobile numbers.
- Printed receipts are core: pledge receipt and payment receipt; the owner chooses which fields print (Loan Number always prints).
- Must stay light and usable on low-end Android phones and slow data connections.
- English now; Hindi/regional languages later, so layouts must tolerate longer labels.
- Reports open behind a password pop-up (owner decision); reports and exports are permission-gated.
- No dashboard page by design: the shop's home is the Loans list; figures live in Reports.
- Undecided: further constraints the owner has said exist ("many more") — to be added as they are named.

## Brand Commitments

Name and the serif wordmark "dhiran." are binding. Visual identity chosen by the owner (2026-11-22): the Bahi-khata — the shop's own red ledger: committed oxblood cloth shell, calm white pages, ledger rhythm on money tables. The owner explicitly dislikes the yellow/gold currently used (scrollbar, buttons, accents: "looks cheap"): NO yellow or gold fills anywhere. The inherited JewelFlow look is an anti-reference: Dhiran must look its own, modern, not a derivative.

## Evidence on Hand

Public pages exist (home, pricing, terms, privacy, refund, contact) with DRAFT legal copy awaiting the owner's review. Plans: yearly and monthly (see pricing page). No customer testimonials, case studies or press exist; none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Accuracy before speed before beauty: a screen never hides or rounds a money figure.
2. Every screen size is a first-class citizen; tablet and phone are designed first.
3. A clerk should never wonder what a button does or why an action was refused; say why, in plain words.
4. Print output is a product surface, not an afterthought.
5. Calm and professional over decorative: this book is opened all day.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast and keyboard support, 44px minimum touch targets, visible focus, reduced-motion respected. Staff are not all tech-comfortable.
